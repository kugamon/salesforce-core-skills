# Credits

## Origin (2026)

This skill was written for the salesforce-core-skills project by Kugamon,
MCP-first from the start. Its shared record-writing canon
(`shared/standards/record-data-quality.md`) began life inside the sf-leads
skill and was promoted when it proved to be universal. The per-object
playbooks draw on the collective knowledge of the Salesforce admin and
consulting community.

## Community Resources

- **Salesforce Developer Documentation** — developer.salesforce.com
- **SOAP/REST API Developer Guides** — merge semantics and record limits
- **Salesforce Help** — Duplicate Management, Matching Rules, Entitlements
  and Milestones, Products and Price Books
- **Salesforce Stack Exchange** — community Q&A on merge behaviour, price
  book entry constraints, and polymorphic ownership
- **Salesforce Ben** — data-quality and administration best-practice articles
